# Data questions before we commit to next quarter's plan

A few numbers in `tandem_data.xlsx` don't line up, and they decide how big the churn problem is. The first four matter most.

## Numbers that don't match

1. **Conversions vs. paying teams.** The funnel shows 976 conversions to paid in 90 days, but there are only 1,400 paying teams in total. Is "converted" counting users or teams? How many paying teams did we gain, net, last quarter?
2. **Retention cohort.** The retention tab covers 420 + 980 = 1,400 teams, which is every paying team. It says it covers only teams that started paying 6 to 12 months ago. Which is it?
3. **Retention vs. churn.** That retention data implies about 9% monthly churn. The churn tab says 3.1% to 4.4%. Which is right, and is churn counted by teams or by revenue?
4. **Exit survey.** 312 people answered in 3 months, but only about 150 to 185 paid teams cancelled. Does the survey include free teams, or several people per team? What do paid teams alone say?

## Sync

5. How many paying teams filed sync tickets, and how many of them later cancelled?
6. Did anything change the ticket count besides the July offline-mode launch, such as user growth or new ticket tags?

## Brightwell and SSO

7. Brightwell's $180k is about 100 times our normal price. What do they need beyond SSO (for example security review, user provisioning, audit logs, or support terms)? Is the commitment signed?

## Engineering capacity

8. How many engineers make up the 36 engineer-weeks, and are they split by web and mobile? The sync fix is 12 weeks of work in a 13-week quarter, so how many people can work on it at once?
