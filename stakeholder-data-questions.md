# Data questions before we commit to next quarter's plan

A few numbers in `tandem_data.xlsx` don't line up, and they decide how big the churn problem is.

1. **Conversions vs. paying teams.** The funnel shows 976 conversions to paid in 90 days, but there are only 1,400 paying teams in total. Is "converted" counting users or teams? How many paying teams did we gain, net, last quarter?
2. **Retention vs. churn.** The retention data implies about 9% monthly churn. The churn tab says 3.1% to 4.4%. Which is right, and is churn counted by teams or by revenue?
3. **Exit survey.** 312 people answered in 3 months, but only about 150 to 185 paid teams cancelled. Does the survey include free teams, or several people per team? What do paid teams alone say?
4. **Sync tickets.** How many paying teams filed sync tickets, and how many of them later cancelled?
